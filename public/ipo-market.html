How to use this IPO screen

Compare the current price with the IPO issue/allotment price, then open the stock chart for the full post-listing history. The page uses a rolling three-year MAINBOARD universe and deliberately excludes SME IPOs. Current price is refreshed from Yahoo Finance daily data. Unavailable price data is shown without removing the IPO from the table.
