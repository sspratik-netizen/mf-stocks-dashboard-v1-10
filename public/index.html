Why a fund is labelled Momentum, Attractive, Watch or Strong Accumulation

Momentum: 30D > 0%, 180D > 5%, 360D > 10% and the fund ranks in the top 2 of its category based on longer-term performance.

Attractive: 30D ≤ -5%, while 180D and 360D remain positive and the fund ranks in the top 2. This identifies a strong fund undergoing a meaningful correction; it does not mean the fund is undervalued.

Strong Accumulation: 30D ≤ -8%, 180D > 5%, 360D > 10% and category rank ≤ 2. This is a stronger correction-with-trend signal.

Watch: 30D ≤ -3% with positive 180D and 360D returns. It needs further confirmation.

Neutral: none of the above conditions is satisfied.

Important: These are systematic screening signals based on returns and category ranking, not guaranteed buy recommendations, valuation calls or personalized investment advice.
