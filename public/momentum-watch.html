How Momentum Stock Watch works

Fund universe: the dashboard selects the top 10 funds for the selected category using the existing category performance ranking. Small Cap and Mid Cap are calculated independently. Only stocks held by at least 2 selected funds are eligible for the consensus list.

Qty Δ: total reported share-count change versus the previous monthly portfolio snapshot, summed across tracked funds where both quantities are available. A positive number means reported shares increased; a negative number means they decreased. This is a portfolio snapshot comparison, not transaction-level buy/sell data.

Avg Allocation: average current portfolio weight among the selected funds that hold the stock. Funds that do not hold the stock are not included.

Δ Allocation: average fund-level allocation change versus the previous month, including funds that exited the stock (their current allocation is treated as 0%). The Fund Flow column shows every tracked fund outcome.

Correction: stock decline from its highest closing price over the preceding ~63 trading days. SMA Up shows the shortest moving average currently above price; longer averages are implied.

Portfolio metadata: the box above the table shows the latest portfolio date used. Monthly portfolio disclosures are generally as of month-end and published within 10 calendar days of month-end. citeturn3search3turn3search12

Watch: a screening flag when a stock has a meaningful recent correction and at least one tracked fund is increasing allocation. This is a watchlist signal, not a buy recommendation.
