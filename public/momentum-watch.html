A 2025 Morgan Stanley/Counterpoint Global study of 6,500+ U.S. stocks found that about 54% never returned to their prior peak after their maximum drawdown; for stocks with a 50–55% maximum drawdown, about 77% eventually regained the prior peak. How Momentum Stock Watch works

Fund universe: the dashboard selects the top 10 funds for the selected category using the existing category performance ranking. Small Cap and Mid Cap are calculated independently. Only stocks held by at least 2 selected funds are eligible for the consensus list.

Avg Allocation: average current portfolio weight among the selected funds that hold the stock. Funds that do not hold the stock are not included.

Δ Allocation: average fund-level allocation change versus the previous month, including funds that exited the stock (their current allocation is treated as 0%). The Fund Flow column shows every tracked fund outcome: increased ↑, decreased ↓, unchanged →, new, or sold/exited.

Correction: stock decline from its highest closing price over the preceding ~63 trading days. SMA Up shows the shortest moving average currently above price; longer averages are implied (20 / 50 / 100 / 200).

Holdings date: the status line shows the latest portfolio disclosure date used for the allocation comparison when the source publishes a date.

Watch: a screening flag when a stock has a meaningful recent correction and at least one tracked fund is increasing allocation. This is a watchlist signal, not a buy recommendation.
