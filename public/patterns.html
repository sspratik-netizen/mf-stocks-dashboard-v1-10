How this scanner works

Universe: current Nifty 500 constituents. NSE describes Nifty 500 as the top 500 companies by full market capitalisation and provides the constituent list.

Timeframe: approximately one year of daily price history. Recent means the detected formation ended within the last 30 trading sessions; Past means an older detected formation within the one-year scan window.

Patterns: Triangle, Head & Shoulders, Wedges, Flags, Channels, Double Top/Bottom and Rounding Top/Bottom. The scanner uses objective price/shape heuristics; it is a screening tool, not a guarantee that a human technical analyst would label every formation exactly the same way.

Important: A pattern is a candidate for further review. Always check volume, breakout/breakdown confirmation, support/resistance and the actual chart before making an investment decision.

Data: NSE Nifty 500 constituent list + Yahoo Finance daily price history.
