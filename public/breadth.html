How to read breadth

Higher percentages indicate broader participation. When breadth expands, more constituents are participating in the move. SMA breadth is the percentage of constituents trading above the selected moving average. RS55 measures 55-trading-day relative outperformance versus the index benchmark.

The page shows the last 30 available trading sessions using the current constituent list. The data is a screening/market-health indicator, not an investment recommendation.
